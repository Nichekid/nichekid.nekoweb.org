 I am still very much alive. 

It's been exactly 84 days, (as of the writing of this blog post) since I last updated this website. Oops. Life got in the way unfortunately, but I have not abandonded this website at all. 

I have still been trying to see if I post my art again, its been over 2 years since I last uploaded any new artwork online, besides the new things I uploaded here on my website. Believe me, i've been wanting to share my artwork online again, as well as continue to maintain this place as my central hub for all of my things. My own handmade corner of the internet I can cutomize any way I want... 

I originally stopped upploading my art online as I used to upload my art on instagram, but after the whole generative AI debacle that's been cooking for the past few years, and the fact that even then, they had changed their terms of service, basically saying, everything you upload is free game for them to feed their LLMS, made me stop using it all together. Any attempts to try to opt-out do not work at all, which is a giant shame. You'll still find my old uploads on there, I mean there's not much I can do with what was already uploaded but at the very least I've been protecting my artwork with [Glaze](https://glaze.cs.uchicago.edu) and with [Nightshade](https://nightshade.cs.uchicago.edu). 

It's already been said many many times before, but what hurts me the most about generative AI is that fact that people value it so much more than art they make with thier owns hands because we live in a world where, for lack of a better word, instant fully sanitized, and appealing "artwork" is always valued more in the corporate world.

There are also those who believe you must be "born" with the gift of drawing, and there are those who believe that if you dont draw "perfectly" you can't consider your self an artist. Art is a skill you build over time. You don't always have to draw for an audience. You should draw for your self too. Just because you want. It's one of the purest forms of expression, expressing things you do with words alone. They do say a picture is worth a thousand words, no?

Not only that, but art is a basic human thing. Creativity, drawing, writing, crafting and eveything that can fall under art is something we did as kids, not because we were trying to sell it, or impress an audience. We did because we wanted to. 

As kids it helped us build our motors skills. It helped us express ourselves, express our love for things, express how we felt, express the way we viewed the world in our innocent eyes. Thats hasn't changed. But we are stripping away kids natural curiosity and creativity by sticikng them infront of screens and showing them that, "Hey! You dont ever have to make anything anymore, or think for yourself, the AI will do it for you!" 

This being concerning is an understatement.

I hope that kids of today still learn how to cherish something made by themselves. So that when they grow older, they don't need to rely on giant corporations to do the "work" for them.
