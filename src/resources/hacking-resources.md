## Helpful Tips:
* VIDEO GUIDES are not recommended! (MOSTLY/ESPECIALLY for NINTENDO console guides.) They are OUTDATED very quickly most of the time and can brick (ruin) your consoles.
* Follow guides to a tee, and feel free to ask the discord and Reddit communities associated with the hacking community for any questions and advice. And search first before asking, there’s a chance someone has had the same issue as you. 
* Make sure you have more storage on them for storing homebrew apps, backups and more! Buy sd cards or see if they use proprietary memory cards.
* Be aware that there’s always a slight chance of bricking (ruin) your console. Again, to avoid this, make sure to follow guides to a tee. Mod at your own risk.
* Some consoles still have active online capabilities. Be aware that hacking them could result in you getting your account and consoles permanently banned. Follow the guides and make sure you are aware of this possibility and how to prevent this. Don’t try to cheat online games or pirate games from active storefronts. Support devs (especially small devs).
*  Don’t go around asking how to get free games in public communities as it can risk getting them in trouble with Nintendo/Sony etc. Figure this out on your own.
* Hacking/Modding your system COULD make them more susceptible to being maliciously infected. Be careful what you download and make sure to use trusted sites and guides!
* Despite the risks, don’t let it scare you into never doing it, modding consoles and joining these communities is incredible, and seeing folks go out of their way to keep things alive that big companies have deemed obsolete is very admirable! 
* (For 3ds and Wii U) DONT SKIP BACKING UP YOUR NAND. All NANDS are unique to each console, and it will be nearly impossible to save the system if it gets bricked.

## FAQ:

### Is hacking/modding/Jailbreaking consoles and systems illegal? 
- Nope! You own the systems and it’s within your right to use them however you like! 

### What’s Dumping? 
- Taking a dum- I mean- dumping means getting physical versions of games and “dumping” them so that you can have the digital files of said game. Dumping games allows you to play digital versions of games you own physically, so that you don’t need to use the cartridge/cd anymore! And if anything were to happen to them, you can forever have copies of your own games you paid for.  

### What’s CFW? 
- Custom firmware! Firmwares that are modified to be able to do things that it couldn’t normally do, plus run unsigned/custom code, and enable homebrew!

### What’s OFW?
- Official firmware. The firmware that comes preinstalled with your console!

### What’s homebrew?
- Unofficial Software that can only run on modded consoles. This could be emulators, programs or even games! 

### What’s an emulator?
- Emulators imitate other systems and consoles, and tricks software to run things made for other systems. This is how you can still play old games on newer systems, and how you can even play old Nintendo games on a Sony Consoles and vice-versa!

### What’s a “bricked” console? 
- A bricked console is a console that is dead. Basically another word for turning it into paperweight. May be recoverable under certain circumstances. (Called a soft-brick in that case.) 

### What’s a ROM? 
- Read only memory file. Contains information from ROM chips. In this context, games! Many games are packaged in ROM chips.

### What’s an ISO? 
- Disc image file. Also, a game! Except this time, it’s stored on a disc, and ISO’s are usually the file on the disc. May also come in .bin format plus more depending on console.  

### What’s a port? 
- A game rebuilt to be played on systems it wasn’t made for! For example, [PSP Cave Story Port](https://www.cavestory.one/download/cave-story.php) or [Sonic Mania PS Vita port](https://github.com/SonicMastr/Sonic-Mania-Vita) or [OFF 3DS Port](https://db.universal-team.net/3ds/off). 

### What’s a “Flashcart”?
- They allow you play roms or mutiple files of several games in one cartridge! Some may also allow you to run other programs and games never officially published. Flashcarts are great for consoles that tehcnically can't have CFW so you can still run custom programs and game backups without hacking! 

### What’s a NAND?
- Holds memory without a power source, Main chips that hold important system data, usually mentioned and associated with the Wii U and 3DS. All Wii U and 3DS nands are different and specifically made for each individual console, which is why backing them up is so important. They cannot be replaced on other consoles.

### Can I make my own software and games on these systems? 
- Yes you can! If you have the know how, you can make your own software for these consoles and run them!
